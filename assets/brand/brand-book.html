01 · The briefBrief and references

A web reader for AI agents, with a face for every gate.

Svipall is an MCP server and CLI in Rust that turns web pages into Markdown, crawls, searches without an API key and attempts captchas locally. Developers connect it to their agents.

02 · The ideaThe watcher under the brim

Svipall is one of Odin’s names: the one who changes, a different face in every place.

He travelled in disguise under a broad hat (Síðhöttr, another of his names) and had given one eye at Mímir’s well. The symbol is that traveller seen from the gate: the brim falls over the side where the eye is missing and lifts over the one that still watches.

— The hat is always ink or paper; only the eye takes alert red.

— Construction: a tapered crown with no pinch, a brim drawn as one curve, an almond eye whose height (u) sets every spacing.

— The myth and the spy film share one silhouette, without a padlock or a screen.
