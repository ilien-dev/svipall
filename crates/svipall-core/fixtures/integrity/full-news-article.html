Council approves the harbour plan

By a staff reporter — 14 March 2026

The council voted on Tuesday to approve the harbour redevelopment after a debate that ran past midnight. Eleven members voted in favour, four against, and two abstained on the grounds that the costings had not been circulated in time to be read.

Supporters argued that the plan was overdue and that the money had been set aside three budgets ago. "We have been talking about this since before I was elected," one member said during the second hour of debate. Opponents said the figures had never been published in full, and that the traffic study relied on counts taken during a school holiday.

A second reading is expected before the end of the month. If it passes, work on the eastern quay would begin in the autumn and run for roughly two years, with the fish market operating from a temporary site on the far side of the basin for the duration.
